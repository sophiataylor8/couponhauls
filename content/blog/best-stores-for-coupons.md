Some stores are coupon goldmines. Others rarely budge on price. After tracking thousands of deals across hundreds of retailers, we've identified the 10 stores that consistently deliver the biggest and most reliable savings through coupon codes and promo offers.

## 1. Kohl's — The Undisputed Coupon King

Kohl's is legendary in the couponing community for a reason. The retailer operates on a near-permanent cycle of:

- **30% off sitewide codes** (often stackable with sale prices)
- **Kohl's Cash** — earn $10 for every $50 spent, redeemable on your next order
- **Buy More Save More** events — up to 25% off when buying multiple items

Kohl's also allows stacking a percentage-off code with Kohl's Cash redemption, making it one of the best places to stack deals. Check our [Kohl's coupons](/kohls/) page for today's active codes.

## 2. Target — Circle Rewards + Weekly Sales

Target's Circle loyalty program is free to join and delivers consistent savings:

- **1% back** on every purchase as a Circle reward
- **Exclusive Circle offers** — often 10–20% off specific categories
- **Weekly ad deals** — price cuts on hundreds of items every Sunday
- **Target RedCard** — an additional 5% off every purchase

The combination of Circle offers, weekly sales, and promo codes regularly yields 25–35% off without much effort.

## 3. Nike — Member Discounts + Seasonal Sales

Nike's own app and website offer member-exclusive access to:

- **15–25% off** during major sale events (Black Friday, end of season)
- **Nike promo codes** for new members
- **Student and military discounts** through ID.me verification

Browse our [Nike coupons](/nike/) for the latest active codes.

## 4. Adidas — Outlet + Promo Code Stacking

Adidas is generous with discounts, regularly offering:

- **30% off** sitewide codes during promotional periods
- **Sale section** with permanent reductions up to 50%
- **adiClub loyalty points** redeemable on future purchases
- **Student discounts** through UNiDAYS

Stack a sitewide code on top of already-reduced outlet items for savings up to 60%.

## 5. Wayfair — Flash Sales + Free Shipping Codes

Home décor shoppers know Wayfair runs aggressive discount cycles:

- **Way Day** (annual sale) — up to 80% off furniture and home goods
- **Flash sales** — 24-48 hour deals on specific categories
- **Free shipping** on orders over $35 (most items qualify anyway)
- Promo codes offering an **extra 10–15%** on top of sale prices

Find today's [Wayfair coupons](/wayfair/) here.

## 6. Walmart — Rollback + Clearance Stacking

While Walmart codes aren't as dramatic as Kohl's, the combination of:

- **Rollback prices** (permanent markdown on popular items)
- **Walmart+ free trial** (free delivery, Paramount+ included)
- **Cashback via Ibotta** (often 5–25% on specific products)
- **Walmart promo codes** for groceries and general merchandise

…makes Walmart one of the best for everyday essentials. See active [Walmart codes](/walmart/).

## 7. Amazon — Lightning Deals + Subscribe & Save

Amazon's discount system is deep once you know how to use it:

- **On-page coupons** — click the checkbox under the price
- **Subscribe & Save** — up to 15% off recurring household items
- **Lightning deals** — time-limited discounts up to 70% off
- **Prime exclusive prices** — lower prices visible only to members

Combine these with a cashback portal for maximum savings. Browse [Amazon coupons](/amazon/).

## 8. Home Depot — Pro Xtra + Seasonal Deals

For home improvement shoppers, Home Depot delivers:

- **10% military discount** year-round
- **Pro Xtra loyalty program** with volume pricing and exclusive offers
- **Spring and Black Friday tool sales** — up to 40% off major brands
- Promo codes for **free delivery** on orders over $45

Check our [Home Depot coupons](/homedepot/) for active savings.

## 9. Walgreens — Digital Coupons + BOGO Offers

Walgreens runs one of the most generous pharmacy reward systems:

- **myWalgreens Cash** — 1–5% back on every purchase
- **Digital coupons** clipped in-app — often 30–50% off health and beauty
- **Buy One Get One 50% off** deals rotate weekly
- **Prescription savings** for myWalgreens members

See active [Walgreens deals](/walgreens/).

## 10. Overstock — Clearance + Code Stacking

Overstock's furniture and home goods are already discounted vs. retail, and the site regularly offers:

- **Extra 10–15% off** sitewide via promo codes
- **Clearance section** with up to 70% off floor models and overstocked items
- **Free shipping** on most orders
- **Club O membership** for 5% back on all purchases

Browse [Overstock coupons](/overstock/) to find today's best codes.
