## Contact CouponHauls

Have a question, found a broken coupon, or want to work with us? We'd love to hear from you!

## How to Reach Us

**Email:** info@couponhauls.com

We typically respond within 1–2 business days. Please include as much detail as possible so we can help you quickly.

## Common Reasons to Contact Us

**Broken or expired coupon?**
We do our best to keep all codes up to date, but sometimes they slip through. If you find a coupon that no longer works, let us know and we'll remove or update it right away.

**Store not listed?**
If you'd like to see a specific store added to our site, send us the store name and website and we'll look into adding it.

**Partnership or advertising inquiry?**
We're open to working with brands and affiliate networks that align with our audience. Reach out and tell us about your offer.

**General feedback?**
We're always looking to improve. Your feedback — good or bad — helps us build a better experience for everyone.
