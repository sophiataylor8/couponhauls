## Affiliate Disclaimer

*Last updated: March 2025*

CouponHauls participates in affiliate marketing programs. This page explains what that means and how it affects you.

---

## What Is Affiliate Marketing?

Affiliate marketing is a way for websites to earn a commission by promoting products or services offered by other companies. When you click on an affiliate link on CouponHauls and make a qualifying purchase, we may receive a small commission from the retailer at **no additional cost to you**.

---

## Our Affiliate Relationships

CouponHauls has affiliate relationships with many of the retailers and stores featured on our site. This includes, but is not limited to, programs such as:

- Amazon Associates
- ShareASale
- Commission Junction (CJ Affiliate)
- Impact Radius
- Rakuten Advertising
- Other individual retailer affiliate programs

---

## How This Affects You

- **No extra cost:** Clicking an affiliate link never increases the price you pay. In fact, our coupon codes are designed to save you money.
- **No obligation:** You are never required to purchase anything by clicking a link on our site.
- **Our editorial independence:** Our editorial decisions — which stores to feature, which deals to highlight — are not influenced by affiliate commissions. We only promote deals we believe offer genuine value to our readers.

## FTC Disclosure Compliance

In accordance with the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) guidelines on endorsements and testimonials, CouponHauls discloses that we may earn compensation from affiliate relationships described above. This disclosure is made in compliance with 16 C.F.R. Part 255.
